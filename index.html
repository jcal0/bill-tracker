Bill Tracker

A personal automation for one user.

Bill Tracker is a private, single-user tool. It reads utility bills the owner has already received and records the amount, billing period and due date in the owner's own Google Sheet.

It requests two Google permissions, both used only for the owner's own data:

  • Read email — to find utility bill notifications in the owner's inbox.
  • Google Sheets — to write the recorded bill details into the owner's spreadsheet.

There is no account system, no sign-up, and no other users.
