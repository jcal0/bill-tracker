Privacy Policy — Bill Tracker

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What this app is

Bill Tracker is a private automation run locally by a single person on their own computer, for their own utility bills. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): used to locate utility bill emails belonging to the app's owner.
  • Google Sheets (read and write): used to record bill details — provider, billing period, amount due and due date — in the owner's own spreadsheet.

How data is stored

All data stays on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials are stored in a local, access-restricted file. Nothing is uploaded to any third-party service.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Retention and deletion

Bill details remain in the owner's spreadsheet until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all access immediately.
